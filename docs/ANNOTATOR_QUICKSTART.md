# Annotator quickstart (template)

> **This is a template, not a handout.** Copy it into the study's own guideline
> packet, replace every `<placeholder>`, add screenshots of *that study's*
> actual form, and delete what does not apply. Keep it to one page — it exists
> so a new annotator gets from invite link to first submission without help.
> Walk through the filled copy live at the kickoff session.
>
> It covers the tool only. What counts as a correct judgment belongs in the
> study's guidelines, next to this page.

---

## 1. Log in (do this before the kickoff session)

1. Open the invite link you received by `<email / chat>`. It takes you to
   `<https://lab-server:8080>` — the only address you ever need; bookmark it.
2. Set your password. Your account is already tied to your email; there is no
   public signup page, so always come back through the bookmark, not the
   invite link.
3. You should see your project, **`<project name>`**. If you see no project, an
   empty one, or the login fails, tell `<study contact>` **before** kickoff —
   not on labeling day.

> Log in once ahead of time even if you will not label yet. Almost every
> access problem shows up at this step and takes minutes to fix in advance.

## 2. Open your project

Click **`<project name>`** on the projects page. The list you see is *your*
assignment: `<N>` items, each judged by you alone. You will not see other
annotators' projects, and that is deliberate — judgments must stay
independent, so please do not compare answers with anyone while the study runs.

Click the first item to start. Label Studio keeps your place: finished items
turn to *Completed*, so you can stop and resume any time.

`<screenshot: the task list, one item highlighted>`

## 3. Label one item

`<screenshot: the labeling screen with the form filled in>`

1. Read `<what to read — e.g. the source text on the left, then the candidate
   answer on the right>`.
2. Answer `<the questions, in order>`. Fields marked required must be filled
   before the form will submit, and some questions only appear once you pick a
   particular answer — that is normal.
3. Press **Submit**. The next item loads automatically.

To change an answer you already submitted, reopen the item and press
**Update**. That is fine and it is tracked — do not create a second annotation
for the same item by other means.

Do not rush and do not linger: the time you spend per item is recorded as part
of the study data, so `<expected pace, e.g. "roughly 2-4 minutes per item">` is
useful, but an honest slow judgment beats a fast guess.

## 4. When something is wrong

- **The item itself is broken** (truncated text, missing file, nonsense input):
  `<what to do — e.g. choose the "unusable item" option and submit>`, or skip
  it and note the item id.
- **You are unsure how to judge it**: apply `<the guideline's tie-break rule>`
  and flag it to `<study contact>` — do not ask another annotator.
- **The tool misbehaves** (page will not load, Submit fails, work seems lost):
  stop, do not re-submit repeatedly, and message `<study contact>` with the
  item id and what you saw.

Contact: `<name, channel, response time>`.

## 5. Finishing

You are done when your project shows `<N>` of `<N>` completed. Tell
`<study contact>`; nothing gets exported until you confirm, and reopening
items after that point needs a heads-up.

Thank you — and please keep the content of these items to yourself
`<until publication / per the study's confidentiality note>`.
